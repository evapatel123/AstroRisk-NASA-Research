Exploratory object categorization

AstroRisk applies K-means to standardized log-transformed diameter, velocity, and miss distance. Categories are assigned from cluster centroids after fitting. They describe the observed dataset only; they are not impact-risk scores or collision probabilities.

What this dashboard can support

Use the dashboard to inspect whether size, velocity, and miss distance show linear or monotonic associations; compare observation periods; examine skewed distributions; and test whether unsupervised categories are stable under different samples.

  • Report both Pearson and Spearman correlations.
  • Use log transforms when variables are strongly right-skewed.
  • Separate descriptive NASA flags from the project's model output.
  • Do not interpret cluster membership as an official hazard classification.
